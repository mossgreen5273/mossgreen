11x11盤で遊ぶヴァイキングのタフル系ゲーム。中央の玉座に王と12体の守備兵、盤の四辺に24体の攻撃兵が並ぶ（この簡易ルールでは玉座やコーナーに特別な制限はない）。すべての駒はチェスのルークと同じく、縦横に何マスでも（駒か盤端に止められるまで）進める。敵の駒を、自分の駒2つで縦か横に挟むと捕獲できる（挟まれた状態に自分から進んでも捕獲されない — 捕獲は挟んだ側の手が成立した瞬間にのみ発生する）。王も他の駒と全く同じ方法で捕獲される。王の軍勢は、王が盤の外周のどこか1マスにでも到達すれば勝利。攻撃軍は、王を捕獲するか、王と残る守備兵全員を隙間なく包囲して盤端への逃げ道を断てば勝利。（青くハイライトされたマスは王が逃げ込める外周。）

A Viking-era Tafl game played on an 11x11 board. The King and 12 defenders start on the central throne; 24 attackers line the four edges of the board (this simplified ruleset places no special restrictions on the throne or corner squares). Every piece slides any number of squares horizontally or vertically, like a chess rook, until blocked by another piece or the edge. An enemy piece is captured by sandwiching it between two of your own pieces along a row or column — moving into such a sandwich yourself is safe; capture only happens the instant the trapping move is made. The King is captured exactly the same way as any other piece. The King's side wins the moment the King reaches any square on the board's outer edge. The attackers win by capturing the King, or by encircling the King and all remaining defenders so tightly that none of them can reach the edge. (The blue-highlighted squares are the King's escape zone.)
