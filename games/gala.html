ルールRules

シュレースヴィヒ=ホルシュタイン地方に伝わる中世のタフル系ゲーム。10x10盤は3つの領域に分かれる：四隅の4x4の「城」（紫）・十字型に伸びる幅2マスの帯・中央2x2の「聖域」（金）。境界線を越える手を「越境」と呼ぶ。各陣営は「ガラ（王）」2体、「コルナ（乱戦戦士）」5体、「ホルサ（騎馬兵）」5体、「カンパ（戦士）」8体を持つ。カンパは自分の本拠の城にいる間は中心へ向かう斜め1マス（初手のみ2マス）しか動けず、城の外では王のように全方向へ1マス動ける（自城に戻ると斜めのみに戻る）。コルナは城の中ではルークのように、十字帯・聖域ではビショップのように動き、境界を越える手では両方を組み合わせられる（越境した瞬間が最初の1マス目なら追加の斜め移動は無制限、そうでなければ1マスだけ）。ホルサはコルナの逆（城でビショップ、外でルーク）。コルナとホルサは境界を越える手でしか敵を捕獲できない。ガラは王のように全方向1マス動けるが、捕獲できるのは境界を越える手のときだけ。コルナ・ホルサは聖域に入れない（通過は可）が、カンパとガラは入れる。

勝利条件：自分のガラ2体を同時に聖域の2マスに置けば「大勝利」。相手のガラを両方とも討ち取り、自分のガラを1体でも聖域に置けば「小勝利」。両陣営とも盤上のガラが尽きれば引き分け。

（城内の駒配置は史料の図版が入手できなかったため、記録されている軍勢構成（ガラ2・コルナ5・ホルサ5・カンパ8）を厳密に保ちつつ、対称的に再構成したものです。）

A medieval Tafl-family game from Schleswig-Holstein. The 10x10 board is divided into three regions: four 4x4 "castles" at the corners (purple), a two-square-wide cross of open ground connecting them, and a central 2x2 "holy area" (gold). Crossing one of these boundaries mid-move is called crossing a "deflection line." Each side has 2 Galas (Kings), 5 Kornas ("berserk warriors"), 5 Horsas ("mounted warriors"), and 8 Kampas ("warriors"). A Kampa inside its own home castle may only step one square diagonally toward the centre (two on its very first move); outside its home castle it moves one square in any direction like a King (reverting to diagonal-only if it returns home). A Korna moves like a Rook inside a castle and like a Bishop in the open cross/centre regions; on a move that crosses a boundary it may combine both — an unlimited diagonal continuation if the crossing happens on its very first step, or just one extra diagonal square otherwise. A Horsa is the mirror image (Bishop in castles, Rook outside). Kornas and Horsas may only capture on a move that crosses a boundary. A Gala moves one square in any direction like a King, but may only capture on a move that also crosses a boundary. Kornas and Horsas may not stop in the holy area (though they may pass through it); Kampas and Galas may enter it freely.

Victory: placing both of your Galas in the holy area at once is a "great victory." Eliminating both enemy Galas while you still have one in the holy area is a "minor victory." If both sides run out of Galas, it's a draw.

(The precise historical arrangement of pieces within each castle wasn't available in the text sources consulted, so the layout here is a symmetric best-effort reconstruction that preserves the documented army composition — 2 Gala / 5 Korna / 5 Horsa / 8 Kampa per side — exactly.)
