ごろごろどうぶつしょうぎAbout Gorogoro Animal Shogi

日本将棋連盟考案。5×6盤、4種類の駒(ライオン=玉、イヌ=金、ネコ=銀、ひよこ=歩)を使用。ネコとひよこは成れます。
Devised by the Japan Shogi Association. Played on a 5×6 board with four piece types: Lion (King), Dog (Gold), Cat (Silver), Chick (Pawn). The Cat and Chick can promote.
成りは敵陣2段目まで。二歩・打ち歩詰めは本将棋と同じく禁止です。自分のライオンが相手の最奥列に達しても勝ち(トライ)。
Promotion is available within the far two ranks. Double pawns and pawn-drop checkmate are forbidden, as in standard shogi. Marching your own Lion into the opponent's farthest rank also wins (a "Try").
