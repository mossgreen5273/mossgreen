暗闇のドップラーDoppler in the Dark

ここは何も見えない夜。矢印キー(WASD、スマホは下のボタン)で、出口の鐘の音を頼りに進もう。
気配は近づくと音が高く、遠ざかると低く聞こえる ―― ドップラー効果で距離を読み取れ。
Space(またはボタン)で「反響」を送ると、周囲の気配が一瞬だけ淡く見える。触れられたらやり直しだ。

It is a night where nothing can be seen. Move with the arrow keys (WASD, or the on-screen buttons on mobile) and follow the sound of the exit bell.
Presences sound higher as they approach and lower as they retreat — read the distance through the Doppler effect.
Press Space (or the button) to send out a "Pulse" that briefly reveals nearby presences. If one touches you, you start over.
