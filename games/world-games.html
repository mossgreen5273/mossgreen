アリマー Arimaa

Stronger pieces push or pull weaker enemies, and mere adjacency can freeze a weaker piece in place. Pieces on trap squares vanish instantly without friendly support. Four steps per turn; get a rabbit home to win.

ナイン・メンズ・モリス Nine Men's Morris

Placement gives way to movement, and reducing the opponent to two pieces wins the game.

A 5x5 board crossed with diagonals, mandatory captures, chain jumps, and a King promotion on the far row that grants free-distance movement and capture.
