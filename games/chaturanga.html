CHATURANGA

Ancient Roots of Shōgi & Chess

Two- and four-player Chaturanga, based on the historical account in "Column One: The Origins of Shōgi" (Chū Shōgi Federation)
1Game Mode
Two-Player Chaturanga
The original war game, King and Minister at the center. Pawns promote to the piece that started behind them.
Four-Player Chaturanga
Four armies, one per corner. Roll the die to see which piece can move — team up with your diagonal ally to capture both enemy kings.
